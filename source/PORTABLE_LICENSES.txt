AstroStack includes third-party software in its private runtime.

Python: the Python Software Foundation license is included as runtime/LICENSE.txt.
NumPy, SciPy, OpenCV, rawpy/LibRaw, tifffile, Astropy, ExifRead, Requests,
PySide6, Shiboken6 and their dependencies retain their respective licenses
and notices in runtime/Lib/site-packages and the associated *.dist-info directories.
Qt/PySide6 is dynamically loaded; the runtime and application files remain separate.
IBM Plex fonts and their license are in app/astrostack/assets/fonts.
The exact versions and file hashes are recorded in BUILD-MANIFEST.json.
